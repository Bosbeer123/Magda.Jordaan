If you die without a valid will, the Intestate Succession Act decides who inherits. Your family does not get a say.

## Who inherits

- **A spouse and no children:** the spouse inherits everything.
- **Children and no spouse:** the children share everything equally.
- **A spouse and children:** the spouse gets a child's share or R250 000, whichever is more, and the children share the rest.

## What it costs your family

The Master appoints an executor, and the family often has to provide security. Minor children's inheritance goes
into the Guardian's Fund until they turn 18.

## What to do this week

Find out where your will is. If you don't have one, that is the first phone call.
